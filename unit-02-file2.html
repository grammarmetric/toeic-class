Unit 2 · Activity file 2 — Student BTactics for TOEIC · role play for page 15

Student A — close this page.

This page has the answers your partner has to ask you for. Only open it if your teacher gave you the Student B role.

B

Your role

You are Barton Donovan, the director. You sent a fax to Mr. Carson about the meeting on August 14, but his fax machine was broken and five pieces of information did not come through.

Mr. Carson (Student A) will phone you and apologize. Answer his questions from the fax below. Do not read the whole fax out. Answer only what he actually asks — if his question isn't clear, say “Sorry, could you say that again?” and make him ask properly. That is the point of the exercise.

Fax Message
Important

Re: August 14 meeting

Mr. Carson,

I am writing to let you know that I will be arriving on 1Tuesday, August 13. I am flying with United Airlines and my plane is scheduled to land at 29:15 p.m. Could you arrange my hotel for me?

The main purpose of my visit is 3the problem with our Accuron Line of watches. We have had many complaints about water damage. We must discuss 4how we can deal with the problem. Please invite 5Rick Smith and Mary Davis also.

Barton Donovan.

The five highlighted parts are the ones missing from Mr. Carson's copy.

?

Quick reference while you talk

He may ask…You answer
When are you arriving? / What date?Tuesday, August 13 — the day before the meeting
What time does your plane land?9:15 p.m., United Airlines
What is the main purpose of your visit?The problem with our Accuron Line of watches — the complaints about water damage
What do we need to discuss in the meeting?How we can deal with the problem
Who else should we invite?Rick Smith and Mary Davis

If he asks something the fax does not answer — for example which hotel, or how long you are staying — make something sensible up and keep the conversation going.

💬

Useful language for answering

Answering the phone
Donovan speaking. / Hello Mr. Carson, what can I do for you?

When you didn't catch the question
Sorry, could you say that again? / I'm afraid I didn't catch that.

Giving the information
I'll be arriving on … / It's scheduled to land at … / The main thing is … / I'd like you to invite …

Checking he has it right
Did you get all that? / Let me repeat that — … / Is there anything else you need?

✓

After the call

Check that Mr. Carson wrote down all five pieces of information correctly. He has a box on his page where he can type them in and check his own answers.

Then swap roles if your teacher asks you to, so you both get practice asking the questions.
