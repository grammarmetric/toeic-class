# Teaching runsheet

Four students, four lessons a week, two hours each. Everything is on screen —
students open a link, you circulate.

**Class link (share once):** https://grammarmetric.github.io/toeic-class/

---

## Before every lesson — 3 minutes

- [ ] Open the lesson page on your own device and **play one audio track** to
      wake the speaker up. Tracks load on first tap, not on page load.
- [ ] Check all four students can open the link and see the pictures.
- [ ] Remind them: **same device every time.** Answers are saved in that
      browser only. A different phone, or clearing browsing data, starts fresh.
- [ ] **Day 3 only:** privately send the Student B link to two students.
      Not on the index, not linked from the lesson — see Day 3 below.

**House rule worth setting on Day 1:** nobody presses a **Check** button until
you say so. The whole value is in them committing to an answer first.

---

## How the pages behave

| Thing | What to know |
|---|---|
| **Check** buttons | Mark that section, jump to the first wrong item, show the book's explanation for every option. |
| **Try again** | Clears the marking, keeps their answers. Use it for a second attempt. |
| **Clear my answers** | Wipes the page. Only for a genuine restart. |
| Reveals (`▸ Possible answers`, `▸ Tapescript`) | Closed by default. Tell them when to open. |
| Timers | Start/Pause/Reset on screen. Turn red at zero — no auto-submit, nothing is lost. |
| Audio | First tap downloads the whole track, then plays. A second or two of "Loading…" is normal. |
| Book scans | Real book artwork. On a phone, pinch to zoom on the long reading texts. |

**Sync tip for listenings:** play the audio **once, from your device, on the
speaker**. If all four press play themselves they drift out of sync and you
lose the shared pause-and-discuss moments.

# Day 1 · Unit 1 — Listening Part 1: Photographs

`unit-01.html` · book pages 9–12 · audio 0:37 / 0:24 / 0:39 / **3:24**

|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Settle | Open the link together. Ask what they already know about Part 1. |
| 0:05 | **A Focus** + Test tip | Read the Focus box aloud. Point out: distractors are wrong **nouns and verbs**. |

| 0:25 | *Follow up* | Two more nouns and verbs each, in the box. Compare in pairs, then open *Possible answers*. |
| 0:32 | **2 Test tactic** | Read the grey box. They write **two sentences per picture**. Circulate — this is the core skill. |
| 0:45 | ▶ audio (0:37) | Play once. After each statement, pause: *how close were you?* Then open the Tapescript. |
| **0:52** | **Break — 8 min** | |
| 1:00 | **3 Tactic practice** | Two minutes per picture to brainstorm and predict, in pairs. |
| 1:15 | ▶ audio (0:24) | Short — play twice. Pause after each. Open *Possible answers & tapescript*. |
| 1:22 | **Understanding natural English** | ▶ (0:39). They type the missing words. → **Check** |
| 1:30 | **B Mini-test** | Exam conditions: silence, one play, 3:24 straight through. |
| 1:36 | Mark it | → **Check my answers**. Go through every wrong one — the page gives the tapescript line *and* why each option fails. |
| 1:50 | **C Learn by doing** | Which picture do the examples describe? → **Check** (it's 3). Then a three-sentence story. |

`unit-05.html` · book pages 24–27 · no audio · two built-in timers

This is the fullest page of the week. **Section C is your overflow** — set it as
homework without guilt if the mini-test discussion runs long.

| Time | Section | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Settle | Homework from Day 1: two students read their three sentences, class picks the correct one. |
| 0:10 | **A Focus** + Test tip | The idea: decide the *part of speech* before you look at the options. |

| 0:47 | Second pass | No timer. Go back to the ones they skipped. → **Check the 2-pass set** |
| **0:55** | **Break — 8 min** | |
| 1:03 | **3 Tactic practice** | A: part of speech for four sentences → **Check parts of speech**. B: the real options → **Check questions 1–4** |
| 1:18 | Tactics checklist | Read it together before the test. |
| 1:20 | **B Mini-test** | ⏱ Start the **6:00** timer. Twelve questions, 2-pass method, guess and move on. |
| 1:27 | Mark it | → **Check my answers**. Focus the discussion on suffixes: *-ly*, *-ment*, *-able*. |

## When something goes wrong

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Audio says *"Could not load — tap to retry"* | Tap play again. Usually classroom wifi dropping the first request. |
| Audio won't start on an iPhone | The student must tap **play** themselves once; iOS blocks audio until they do. |
| A student's answers vanished | Different browser, different device, or cleared data. Nothing is stored on a server. Have them redo the section — it's fast. |
| Page looks tiny / text unreadable | It's a scan of the real book page. Pinch to zoom; the answer buttons underneath stay full size. |
| Someone pressed **Check** too early | **Try again** clears the marking and keeps their answers. |
| Wrong student opened the Student B page | Only affects that one role play. Swap the pairs around. |
| A student is way ahead | Send them to the *Further study* box, or the reveals they haven't opened. |

---

## Marking and records

There are **no records** — by design. Nothing is sent anywhere, nothing is
collected. If you want a score, ask them to read it off the screen, or glance at
their device as you circulate.
