Five-minute warm-up

Do not read the passage.

Reading Part 7 · Reading Comprehension

Every instinct you have about reading tests is wrong for this part. That is the lesson.

Why not

You are not being tested on the passage. You are being tested on finding things in it.

Read it all first

Four minutes reading. Then you read it again anyway, looking for each answer.

Questions first

Thirty seconds reading the questions. Then one pass, hunting for words you already know you need.

Same passage. Half the time.

The four types, in the order you should do them

1 · Specific information

What time does the office open? One sentence answers it. Fastest — do these first.

2 · Vocabulary

The word “robust” is closest in meaning to… Quick. Guess and move on if you don’t know it.

3 · Main idea / inference

Why is Mr. Jones writing? Easier once you have already been through the text for 1 and 2.

4 · NOT questions

Which of the following is NOT true? You must check all four choices. Slowest — leave till last.

The numbers are the order. They are not the difficulty.

Worked example — sort these

Which would you answer first?

  • “Who most likely sent this e-mail?”
  • “How much is the delivery charge?”
  • “Which service is NOT offered?”
  • “The word ‘prompt’ in line 4 is closest in meaning to…”

Second, fourth, first, third — in that order. The delivery charge is one line of scanning; the NOT question is four.

How to scan

Underline the nouns and verbs. Ignore everything else.

“By when must notice be given to receive the maximum refund?”

Three words. Now your eye runs down the page looking for notice, given, refund — not reading, hunting.

The one that costs people marks

A NOT question is four questions wearing one number.

Ordinary question

Find one thing. Stop.

NOT question

Check A. Check B. Check C. Check D. Then answer.

Do it last, when you have already been through the passage three times for the others — half the checking is done.

Your turn — out loud

Name the type.

  • “What is the purpose of this notice?”
  • “Where should completed forms be sent?”
  • “Which of the following is NOT required?”

Main idea (do third). Specific information (do first). NOT (do last). Say the number, not the name.
