Five-minute warm-up

The answer is often in a different sentence.

Reading Part 6 · Text Completion

That one fact is the whole unit. Everything else follows from it.

What happens

A short business text with gaps in it.

The text

A letter, a memo, an email, a notice.

The gaps

Several per text, four options each.

Looks like

Part 5 — but it is not.

In Part 5 the sentence contains everything you need. In Part 6, sometimes it simply does not.

Here is the trap

Read only this sentence and you cannot answer.

Since then, we ……… over two hundred new customers.
Asign
Bsigned
Chave signed
Dwill sign

Three of these are grammatically fine. You need the sentence before it.

Now read the sentence before

Our new branch opened last March.
Since then, we ……… over two hundred new customers.
Asignpresent
Bsignedfinished past
Chave signedpast → now ✓
Dwill signfuture

Last March … since then — started in the past, still true now.

What to hunt for

Time words fix the tense.

Past

last week · in 2019 · yesterday · before

Now

currently · still · as we speak · these days

Future

next month · by Tuesday · we are planning to

Underline them as you read. They are doing the question for you.

Not only tenses

Some gaps are joining words.

It wasn't the largest company. It was, ………, one of the most respected.
Afurthermoreadds
Bthereforeresult
Chowevercontrast ✓

Ask: is the second idea the same direction as the first, or the opposite? Here it is the opposite.

The routine

  • Read the whole text quickly first — do not stop at gap one.
  • At each gap, ask: what kind of word is missing?
  • No clue in the sentence? Look before it and after it.
  • Still stuck? Guess and move on. Part 7 needs your time.
